Dice of Kalma

Producer - 2026

As part of my university degree, I worked as the producer on Dice of Kalma during Game Project 3, in collaboration with Pepperbox Studios, supporting the development of additional content and marketing deliverables.

I led a team of 7 across 4 sprints, managing the project in Jira and running sprint planning, stand-ups, retrospectives, and weekly meetings to maintain steady progress.

I acted as the main point of contact with Pepperbox Studios, ensuring clear communication, aligning on requirements, and incorporating feedback. I also coordinated tasks, tracked progress, and maintained production documentation, including requirements and risk tracking.

I monitored timelines, adapted to changing priorities, and ensured milestones were delivered on time. I also presented progress and deliverables to stakeholders.

This experience strengthened my ability to manage external collaboration and deliver structured, on-time results.

Delve Deep

Producer - 2025

As part of my university degree, I worked on Delve Deep during Game Project 2, a dungeon crawler combining elements of Diablo and XCOM.

I led a team of 7 over a 4-month period, using Basecamp to manage the project. I organised weekly meetings and bi-weekly sprints, maintaining structure and tracking progress throughout development.

Our goal was to deliver a dungeon prototype and an overworld prototype. We successfully delivered both, leaving the project in a strong position for continued development.

As the producer, I developed my skills in sprint planning, team coordination, and scope management. I learned to manage team dynamics and maintain motivation during uncertain stages, while adapting my role in later stages to support playtesting and bug tracking.

Slug Wars
