Dice of Kalma

Game Project 3

Producer - 2026

As part of my university degree, I worked as the producer on Dice of Kalma during Game Project 3, in collaboration with Pepperbox Studios, supporting the development of additional content and marketing deliverables.

I led a multidisciplinary team of 7 across 4 sprints, managing the project in Jira and organising sprint planning, stand-ups, retrospectives, and weekly progress meetings to maintain steady development.

I acted as the main point of contact between our team and Pepperbox Studios, ensuring clear communication, aligning on requirements, and incorporating feedback throughout the project. I also coordinated task allocation, tracked progress, and maintained production documentation, including requirements and risk tracking.

Throughout development, I monitored timelines, adapted to changing priorities, and helped maintain project momentum to ensure milestones were delivered on time. I also presented progress and deliverables to stakeholders, communicating key updates and the overall state of the project.

This experience strengthened my ability to manage external communication, maintain clarity across teams, and support structured, efficient development in a collaborative environment.

Delve Deep

Game Project 2

Producer - 2025

As part of my university degree, I worked on Delve Deep during Game Project 2, a dungeon crawler combining elements of Diablo and XCOM.

I led a multidisciplinary team of 7 over a 4-month period, using Basecamp to manage the project. I organised weekly meetings and bi-weekly sprints, maintaining clear structure and tracking progress throughout development.

As the project is ongoing, our goal was to deliver two key features: a fully functional dungeon prototype and an overworld prototype. We successfully delivered both, leaving the project in a strong position for continued development.

As the producer, I developed my skills in sprint planning, team coordination, and scope management while keeping the project on schedule. I learned to manage different personalities and maintain team motivation, even during uncertain stages of development. I also adapted my role in later stages, focusing more on playtesting and bug tracking.

Slug Wars

Hovi Production

Assitant Producer - 2024–2025
